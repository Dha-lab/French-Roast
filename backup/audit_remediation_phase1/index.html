THE ART OF OUR BLEND

Know Your Coffee

Every ingredient brings something distinctive to the cup. Explore the characteristics of Arabica and Robusta coffee beans, how roasted chicory contributes to heritage blends that include it, and how they compare across flavour, body, and caffeine.

COFFEE BEAN Coffea Arabica

Arabica

AROMA · SWEETNESS · COMPLEXITY

Prized worldwide for its nuanced cup profile, Arabica is characterized by delicate sweetness, fine acidity, and remarkable aromatic complexity. Depending on origin, cultivation altitude, processing method, and roast profile, it can express vibrant notes of fruit, floral aromas, chocolate, caramel, or roasted nuts.

Origin & Roast Variation

Flavour expression is not static; it varies according to bean origin, regional terroir, wash or natural processing, and roasting degree.

Contribution to Blend

Adds aromatic depth, sweetness, nuanced complexity, and a smooth cup finish.

Pure coffee bean • Naturally contains caffeine
COFFEE BEAN Coffea Canephora

Robusta

STRENGTH · BODY · INTENSITY

Robusta delivers a typically stronger, more intense flavour profile backed by a distinctly fuller, heavier body. It is renowned for its assertive roasted, nutty, cocoa, and earthy notes, lending bold presence and helping generate dense, long-lasting crema.

Caffeine Strength

Contains a generally higher caffeine content than Arabica by equal bean weight (roughly double), giving it robust vitality and boldness.

Contribution to Blend

Adds bold structure, full mouthfeel, rich crema formation, and deep roasted intensity.

Pure coffee bean • High caffeine strength
ROASTED ROOT Cichorium Intybus

Chicory

EARTHY DEPTH · BITTERSWEET · TRADITION

Chicory comes from the cultivated root of the chicory plant rather than a coffee bean. When dried and roasted, it develops rich, earthy, woody, and bittersweet flavours with a naturally thick, comforting mouthfeel that pairs smoothly with milk.

Coffee Heritage & Traditions

Employed in historic coffee traditions worldwide, including the famous New Orleans style associated with Café du Monde. Chicory is naturally caffeine-free.

Contribution to Blend

Adds distinctive roasted depth, velvety mouthfeel, and bittersweet richness in blended recipes.

Roasted botanical root • Naturally 100% caffeine-free
HOW OUR INGREDIENTS COMPARE

Understanding Key Differences

A concise side-by-side comparison across botanical origin, flavour complexity, body, caffeine, and blending role.

Aspect Arabica Robusta Chicory
Botanical Source Coffee bean (*Coffea arabica*) Coffee bean (*Coffea canephora*) Roasted root (*Cichorium intybus*), not a coffee bean
Typical Flavour Aromatic complexity, sweetness; fruity, floral, nutty, or chocolatey notes Stronger, bolder; earthy, roasted nut, woody, or dark cocoa notes Earthy, woody, roasted, and bittersweet flavour profile
Body & Bitterness Medium silky body, pleasant acidity, delicate bitterness Full, heavy body, low acidity, pronounced roasted bitterness Heavy, syrupy mouthfeel, zero acidity, pleasant bittersweet depth
Caffeine Contains caffeine (approx. 1.2%–1.5% by bean weight) Generally higher caffeine (approx. 2.2%–2.7% by bean weight) Naturally caffeine-free (0% caffeine)
Role in Blends Aromatic nuance, sweetness, fine acidity, and cup balance Strength, heavy body, structural backbone, and dense crema Earthy depth, body, and traditional richness in heritage blends (e.g. Café du Monde)
• Product Accuracy & Sensory Note: Our French Roast customizable selections are crafted from 100% pure roasted Arabica and Robusta coffee beans. Roasted chicory is highlighted separately for heritage insight and educational context. Characteristic tasting notes describe typical profiles and are not guaranteed for every bean or roast.
